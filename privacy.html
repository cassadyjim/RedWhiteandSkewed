Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 16, 2026

The short version: Red, White, & Skewed does not collect, store, or share any personal information about our visitors.

Who We Are

Red, White, & Skewed is a media literacy website that presents news stories from multiple political perspectives. We are a project dedicated to exposing media spin and encouraging critical thinking.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from visitors to our website. Specifically:

  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • We do not require account registration
  • We do not track individual users

Voting Feature

Our site includes an anonymous voting feature that allows visitors to indicate which perspective they support. This voting system:

  • Does not collect any personally identifiable information
  • Uses a browser fingerprint solely to prevent duplicate voting
  • Does not store or transmit any personal data
  • Cannot be used to identify individual users

Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external news sources and websites. When you click on a link to leave our site, you will be directed to a third-party website. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit.

Web Hosting

Our website is hosted by third-party hosting providers who may collect standard server logs (such as IP addresses and access times) as part of their normal operations. We do not access or use this data for any purpose.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of the website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us
