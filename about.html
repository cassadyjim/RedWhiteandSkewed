Our Mission

Why We Exist

Red, White, and Skewed was born from a simple observation: Americans are living in completely different information realities. A story that outrages one half of the country is celebrated by the other. Facts that seem indisputable to some are dismissed as propaganda by others.

Our media ecosystem has fractured into partisan echo chambers that reinforce existing beliefs while demonizing opposing viewpoints. Most people never see how "the other side" covers the news.

Our solution is radical transparency. Every day, we take one of the most divisive news stories and show you exactly how it's being spun by partisan media on both sides. We present the most aggressive conservative framing alongside the most aggressive liberal framing—not to endorse either view, but to expose how the same facts can be weaponized into completely different narratives.

Then we do something neither side wants: we fact-check both of them. We call out the spin, verify the claims, and provide the context that partisan outlets conveniently omit.

💡 What We Believe

1.

Media bias is real—on both sides. Conservative outlets spin stories to favor conservative narratives. Liberal outlets spin stories to favor liberal narratives. Pretending otherwise is naive.

2.

You can handle the truth. We trust our readers to see both perspectives and think for themselves. We don't need to protect you from opposing viewpoints.

3.

Transparency beats objectivity. No source is truly objective. The best we can do is be transparent about our methods, sources, and reasoning—and let you decide.

4.

Democracy requires informed citizens. You can't participate meaningfully in democracy if you only understand one side's arguments. Even if you disagree with the other side, you should know what they actually believe—not a strawman version.

5.

Critical thinking is a skill. By exposing how media manipulation works, we hope to help readers become better at spotting bias in all their news consumption—including ours.

⚠️ What We Are NOT

We are not "neutral" or "centrist." We have our own biases (everyone does). We try to be fair, but we don't pretend to be robots. When the facts clearly favor one side, we say so.

We are not "both sides are the same." Sometimes one side is more wrong than the other. Our fact-check section doesn't artificially balance criticism—it goes where the evidence leads.

We are not affiliated with any political party, campaign, or organization. We don't take money from political groups. We don't endorse candidates. We exist solely to expose how media works.

We are not the final word. We encourage you to read our sources, check our work, and form your own conclusions. If we get something wrong, tell us.

The Team

Red, White, and Skewed is an independent project created by just a guy who wants to see healthy debate return to American discourse. I got tired of only hearing one side's version of events and wanted a simple way to understand how the same story looks through different lenses. This site is my attempt to bridge that gap—not by telling you what to think, but by showing you what both sides are actually saying.
